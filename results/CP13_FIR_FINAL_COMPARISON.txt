PYTHIA NOTE:
Pythia/Scooby is configured as an L2C prefetcher with
l2c_prefetcher_force_prefetch_at_llc=true. Therefore its L2C filled/useful
counters are not the appropriate final fill location; the LLC counters
capture actual prefetch fill/use behavior.

BERTI NOTE:
Berti internal prefetch-issued counters and cache-level L1D prefetch-issued
counters represent different counting stages and are preserved separately.

COMPATIBILITY NOTE:

Athena trace-consumption path. The compatibility binary used for all CP13
matched results activates Athena's already-present intended pseudo-register
filters. The original frozen Athena source remains untouched.
